SeqSeec

Works out whether an RNA-seq library is stranded, and which way, from the lab notes and a local check of the reads. It then gives the matching settings for featureCounts, Salmon, HISAT2 and other tools. Built for analysts who know the computing side better than the bench.

Replay of saved runs.

Five example runs

Every run on this page was produced on 2026-11-19 by seqseec run --format json. The lab notes are synthetic. The reads are simulated with known orientation, and the R1/R2 swap is deliberate. Reads never leave the machine; only the notes, registry quotes and numeric summaries are sent to the LLM.

Research use only. Not a diagnostic tool. A person reviews every result before using the settings.
